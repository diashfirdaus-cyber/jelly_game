HOW TO PLAY

Take the leap.
Find your feet in the clouds.

01 Find your footing

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On mobile, use the left virtual joystick.

02 Two more jumps in midair

Press Space to jump, then release and press again. Space out your jumps to travel farther.

03 Catch a mushroom boost

Land on a pink mushroom to launch upward and refill all three air jumps.

04 Watch the obstacles

Jump over spinning bars. Moving islands carry you along. Orange platforms briefly crumble away.

Collect crystals and race to the finish. Flags save your checkpoint. Press R to respawn or Esc to pause. Switching art modes keeps your progress.
